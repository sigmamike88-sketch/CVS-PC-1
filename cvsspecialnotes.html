🩸 Histology of Circulatory System
🔹 General Structure of Blood Vessels 🩸
Most blood vessels have 3 tunics (layers):
1. Tunica Intima (Interna)
🔻
Innermost layer facing lumen.
Made of:
✔️ Simple squamous epithelium (endothelium)
✔️ Basement membrane
✔️ Subendothelial loose connective tissue
✔️ Internal elastic lamina (mainly arteries)
2. Tunica Media
🔻
Middle layer.
Contains:
✔️ Smooth muscle cells
✔️ Elastic fibers
✔️ Reticular fibers
🚨 Controls vessel diameter and blood pressure.
3. Tunica Adventitia (Externa)
🔻
Outer connective tissue layer.
Contains:
✔️ Collagen fibers
✔️ Elastic fibers
✔️ Nerves
✔️ Vasa vasorum (in large vessels)
🫀 Histology of Heart
🔹 The Heart Wall
The heart wall has 3 layers:
1 Endocardium
🔻
Equivalent to vascular tunica intima.
Structure:
✔️ Simple squamous endothelial cells
✔️ Thin connective tissue layer
✔️ Elastic + collagen fibers
✔️ Some smooth muscle cells
Subendocardial layer contains:
✔️ Blood vessels
✔️ Nerves
✔️ Purkinje fibers
2 Myocardium
🔻
Thickest layer of heart wall.
🔻
Contains: Cardiac muscle cells, Conducting system cells.
🔻
Thickness: Ventricles > atria. Left ventricle is thickest because it pumps systemic circulation.
🔻 Purkinje Fibers ⚡
✔️ Specialized cardiac muscle cells.
✔️ Located in subendocardial region.
✔️ Conduct electrical impulses through the heart.
3 Epicardium
🔻 Outer layer of heart.
🔻 Equivalent to visceral layer of serous pericardium.
Covered by:
✔️ Mesothelium (simple squamous epithelium)
Contains:
✔️ Loose connective tissue
✔️ Fat cells
✔️ Coronary vessels
✔️ Nerves
💥
Function:
Produces lubricating fluid → reduces friction during heart movement.
🔹 Cardiac Valves
Mainly composed of:
✔️ Dense connective tissue
✔️ Covered by endothelium
✔️ Connected to fibrous skeleton of heart.
✔️ Chordae tendineae anchor valve leaflets.

✔️ Carry blood away from heart
✔️ Thick walls
✔️ Lumen smaller compared with veins.

🔹 Arteries
General Features
✔️ Carry blood away from heart
✔️ Thick walls
✔️ Lumen smaller compared with veins.
Types:
1 Elastic Arteries (Conducting Arteries)
Examples:
🔻 Aorta
🔻 Pulmonary artery
Features:
🔻 Largest arteries (>10 mm)
🔻 Yellow color due to elastin.
Tunica Intima:
😵 Thick
😵 Endothelium
😵 Thick subendothelial connective tissue
Tunica Media:
Most important feature:
✔️ Many elastic lamellae
✔️ Smooth muscle layers
Function:
✔️ Stretch during systole
✔️ Recoil during diastole
✔️ Maintains continuous blood flow
Adventitia:
Contains:
✔️ Vasa vasorum
✔️ Sympathetic nerves
2 Muscular Arteries (Distributing Arteries)
Examples:
✔️ Femoral artery
✔️ Brachial artery
Function:
✔️ Distribute blood to organs.
Histology:
Intima:
✔️ Endothelium
✔️ Subendothelial CT
✔️ Prominent internal elastic lamina
3 Small Arteries
Diameter:
✔️ 0.1–2 mm
Features:
✔️ Thin wall
✔️ Narrow lumen
Media:
✔️ Up to 8 layers smooth muscle
✔️ No external elastic lamina
🧠 Arterial Sensory Receptors
⭐ Carotid Sinus
Function:
✔️ Detects blood pressure changes.
Contains:
✔️ Baroreceptors
✔️ Sensory fibers from CN IX (glossopharyngeal nerve)
Response:
⬆️ BP → stretch receptor activation → brain response → vasodilation → ⬇️BP
Glomus Bodies
🔻
Chemoreceptors.
🔻
Detect blood chemistry:
👉 Oxygen
👉 Carbon dioxide
👉 pH
🩸 Capillaries
General Features
✅ Smallest blood vessels
✅ Site of exchange of gases, nutrients & wastes
✅ Wall consists only of endothelium + basement membrane
1 Continuous Capillaries 🔒
Features:
👉 No pores (fenestrae)
👉 Continuous basal lamina
Found in:
🔻 Muscle
🔻 Connective tissue
🔻 CNS
🔻 Exocrine glands
2 Fenestrated Capillaries 🪟
Features:
👉 Have pores (fenestrae)
👉 Continuous basal lamina
👉 Rapid exchange
Found in:
🔻 Kidney
🔻 Intestine
🔻 Endocrine glands
🔻 Pancreas
3 Sinusoidal Capillaries 🩸
Features:
👉 Large irregular lumen
👉 Large gaps between cells
👉 Discontinuous basal lamina
Found in:
🔻 Liver
🔻 Spleen
🔻 Bone marrow
🔻 Placenta
🔵 Veins
General Features
✅ Carry blood toward the heart
✅ Thin walls
✅ Large lumen
✅ Often contain valves
Types of Veins
1 Venules
📍 Smallest veins
Functions:
👉 Drain capillaries
👉 Site of leukocyte migration during inflammation ☢️
2 Small Veins
👉 Thin intima
👉 Well-developed adventitia
3 Medium Veins
📍 Femoral vein, cephalic vein
Features:
👉 Thin media
👉 Prominent adventitia
👉 Valves present
4 Large Veins
📍 Superior vena cava (SVC)
📍 Inferior vena cava (IVC)
📍 Portal vein
Features:
👉 Thick adventitia
👉 Thin media
